# 10 CFR §50.41 — Additional standards for class 104 licenses

In determining that a class 104 license will be issued to an applicant, the Commission will, in addition to applying the standards set forth in [[10 CFR §50.40]] be guided by the following considerations:

## (a)

The Commission will permit the widest amount of effective medical therapy possible with the amount of special nuclear material available for such purposes.

## (b)

The Commission will permit the conduct of widespread and diverse research and development.
