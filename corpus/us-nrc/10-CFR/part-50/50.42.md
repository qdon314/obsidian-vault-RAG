# 10 CFR §50.42 — Additional standard for class 103 licenses

In determining whether a class 103 license will be issued to an applicant, the Commission will, in addition to applying the standards set forth in [[10 CFR §50.40]], consider whether the proposed activities will serve a useful purpose proportionate to the quantities of special nuclear material or source material to be utilized.
