# 10 CFR §50.45 — Standards for construction permits, operating licenses, and combined licenses

## (a)

An applicant for an operating license or an amendment of an operating license who proposes to construct or alter a production or utilization facility will be initially granted a construction permit if the application is in conformity with and acceptable under the criteria of [[10 CFR §50.31]] through 50.38, and the standards of [[10 CFR §50.40]] through 50.43, as applicable.

## (b)

A holder of a combined license who proposes, after the Commission makes the finding under [[10 CFR §52.103]] of this chapter, to alter the licensed facility will be initially granted a construction permit if the application is in conformity with and acceptable under the criteria of [[10 CFR §50.30]] through 50.33, [[10 CFR §50.34]], [[10 CFR §50.34a]] through 50.38, the standards of [[10 CFR §50.40]] through 50.43, as applicable, and [[10 CFR §52.79]] and 52.80 of this chapter.
