# 10 CFR §50.78 — Facility information and verification

## (a)

In response to a written request by the Commission, each applicant for a construction permit or license and each recipient of a construction permit or a license shall submit facility information, as described in [[10 CFR §75.10]] of this chapter, on IAEA Design Information Questionnaire forms and site information on DOC/NRC Form AP-A and associated forms;

## (b)

As required by the Additional Protocol, shall submit location information described in [[10 CFR §75.11]] of this chapter on DOC/NRC Form AP-1 and associated forms; and

## (c)

Shall permit verification thereof by the International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA) and take other action as necessary to implement the US/IAEA Safeguards Agreement, as described in part 75 of this chapter.
